# A YOOzeCase Company

**Premium Plugins, Extensions & Add-ons for WordPress, Joomla & YOOtheme Pro**

A YOOzeCase Company is a small software studio from Rotterdam, the Netherlands, that builds elements and plugins for YOOtheme Pro on Joomla and WordPress. Joe and Denver run it, and together they have built more than 50 websites with YOOtheme Pro. Every product starts from a real use case, which is also where the name comes from.

Agencies, freelancers and developers who build sites with YOOtheme Pro on Joomla or WordPress. If you have a use case that the standard elements don't cover, [tell us about it](mailto:info@yoozecase.com).

## FAQ

**Do the extensions work on both Joomla and WordPress?**
Most YOOtheme Pro elements come in a Joomla and a WordPress version. Smart Grid and Traffic Boost Indexing are Joomla only for now.

**Which license do you use?**
GPL-2.0-or-later, for paid and free products alike.

**How do I get updates?**
Through the built-in updater of Joomla or WordPress, the same way you update everything else on your site.

**Can I try a product before I buy it?**
Yes. Every product page links to a live demo.

**Why is there a joke in my plugin?**
Because we think humor and serious work go well together. Some products contain an easter egg. One of them is nothing but an easter egg.
